Calibration uses the stage's seen-class population, including current-class all-negative rows. Fit on the other two folds and evaluate the held-out fold. Freeze each task's coefficients for later stages. The reference fixed, unregularized BCE, and 10%-bounded BCE outcomes must reproduce exactly on the same split and folds.

The AP candidate jointly chooses reliable Person/Face shares and unreliable Person share. Full gets the remainder and unavailable Face always has zero weight. Search all 4,851 feasible 5-percentage-point grid candidates, including vertices, then the feasible 1-point grid within ±5 points per coordinate of the winning coarse point. Score mixed-cohort macro AP on current classes with the official sigmoid/ranking convention. Break ties (mAP tolerance 1e-12) by lower fitting BCE, then lexicographic coordinate order. No R1 anchor or initialization enters the AP search. CPU candidate batches of 64. Fit full Validation and save locked weights before reading Test. Same-batch Val and Test report all four methods, final/average mAP and reliability groups. The predeclared Val screen remains at least two folds improving both mAPs and positive mean deltas for both. No network or checkpoint training.
